# Sources and references

The simulator is an original model built from code. These sources informed it; none of their images or text are
redistributed.

## Service documentation (strongest)

- **US Army TM 10-601, Organizational Maintenance: Underwood Standard Typewriters** (1955), text PDF hosted by Old
  Bob's Old Typewriters:
  https://oldbobsoldtypewriters.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Underwood-Standard-Service-Manual-TM-10-601_text.pdf
  Used for: carriage shift (§3; §54–55, the subcarriage is lifted by the shift); the escapement trip (§24: key lever →
  sublever → type bar; the heel pushes the universal bar; the rocker tilts; the loose dog releases a tooth to the
  rigid dog; the rigid dog releases it as the rocker returns); pica = 10 letters per inch (§28a); rack → pinion →
  escapement wheel, with a pinion pawl that freewheels on return (§28c); line space 1, 2 or 3 by pawl and cylinder
  ratchet (§V); ribbon cover: black raises the upper half, red the lower half, stencil no lift (§43); bell 7 spaces
  before the line lock (§XIII d(8)); backspace pawl acting on the rack (§IX).
  Caveat: the manual covers later Underwood standards (serials 4,280,000–5,800,000). The mechanisms cited belong to the
  same family architecture as the No. 5; individual part shapes differ.
- **AMES OAMI Mechanical Training Manual** (index on Typewriter Database), "rack, pinion and starwheel" escapement:
  https://typewriterdatabase.com/1945-AMES-OAMI-MechanicalTrainingMan.index.manual

## Museum and collection records

- Museums Victoria, Underwood Model 5 (388645): 42 typebars in a curved typebasket, front strike, four-row 42-key
  keyboard, ribbon spools on vertical axes: https://collections.museumsvictoria.com.au/items/388645
- Science Museum Group, Underwood typewriter No. 5 (co38184): ~252 mm (10 in) platen, production c.1900–1932:
  https://collection.sciencemuseum.org.uk/objects/co38184/underwood-typewriter-no-5-made-in-nyc-early-20th-century-typewriter
- Science Museum Group, Underwood 1 (co38171), the Wagner front-strike lineage:
  https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co38171
- Xavier University POLT collection, Underwood No. 5 (84 characters): https://site.xavier.edu/POLT/TYPEWRITERS/underwood5.html

## Patents

- US 848,520 (H. L. Wagner, 1907): type-bars pivoted below the platen striking its front side:
  https://patents.google.com/patent/US848520A/en
- US 1,163,801 and related Underwood patents: a type-bar-actuated universal bar vibrating an escapement rocker that
  carries holding and stepping dogs: https://patents.google.com/patent/US1163801A/en
